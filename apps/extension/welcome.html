Point your agent at any page

Drag your agent from your APC dock or Float onto anything on a web page, and ask it about "this". The extension reads the block of text you dropped it on and hands it to your agent through your dock.

1. Let pages take your agent

So any page can notice the drop, the extension needs access to the pages you visit. It does nothing with them until you drop your agent on one: it only listens for that drop. Chrome will warn that it can "read and change" your data on all sites; you can turn this off at any time.

Done: you can drop your agent on any page.

Prefer to choose site by site? Skip this, then use the extension's icon on each site you want.

2. When to ask you

3. The first drop

  1. Drag your agent from the dock or Float onto a paragraph.
  2. The first time, your dock asks you to allow this browser: click Allow there.
  3. Ask your agent "what is this?".

What you drop your agent on goes to your agent through your dock, which keeps it 10 minutes and never logs it. Your agent is told it is page text, never instructions.
