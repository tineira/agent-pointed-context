How it works

Inside a point.

Three channels connect what you see to what your agent can read.

View

The app names your screen, filters and selection with identifiers. No pixels travel.

Pin

Drop your agent on an item. The app gives it the exact tool call that reads that item.

Presence

Your agent is a mark on the page. What it reads lights up as it goes.

Access and privacy

Your agent.
Your permission.

Only the item you point at

APC sends identifiers. Your agent reads the item with the access you gave it. Your screen and screenshots stay out of the exchange.

One connection per app

Each app keeps its data and provides its own MCP address. Your agent brings the answers together in your conversation.

Revoke in the app

Each app lists your agents. Revoke one there and its access to that app ends at once.

The standard

Free, open and versioned.

APC started as a research paper. The spec is frozen and changes only by its versioning policy.

Build it

Make your app pointable.

An APC app serves its own remote MCP endpoint. The person connects an agent to your app, and the agent reads through the tools you already have. Your app keeps its data.

Integrate your app in an afternoon → Read the spec

What to implement

  1. Serve an MCP endpoint

    Give each agent a grant and an MCP URL. Serve get_current_view and wait_for_focus beside your own tools.

  2. Make things pointable

    Mark charts, rows and paragraphs with data-focus: ids and keys only. A drop becomes a focus that names the call that reads it, in next and next_args.

  3. Publish apc.json and offer Revoke

    /.well-known/apc.json says what your app is. List the person's agents in your page, with Revoke.

  4. Pass the conformance suite

    @tineira/apc-conformance checks the view, the pin, the focus format and revoke through your endpoint.
