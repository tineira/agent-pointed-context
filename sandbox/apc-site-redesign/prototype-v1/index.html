    How it works

    Three small channels. Identifiers only.

    The app never sends your screen. It tells your agent what you pointed at, and your agent reads it through the app's own tools.

    1 · The view

    The app says what you see

    As identifiers only: the screen, the period, the row you selected. Never the numbers or the text on it.

    2 · The pin

    You point

    Drag your agent's mark from the app's page onto a bar, a row or a paragraph. On a phone, tap it there. The app gives your agent the exact tool call that reads it: next and next_args.

    3 · Presence

    Your agent reads it

    It calls that tool with the access you gave it. Your agent is a mark on the page, and what it reads lights up, so you see what it looked at.

    Build it

    Make your app pointable.

    APC is an open standard over MCP. Your app keeps its data and answers the person's agent with just what they pointed at, through its own tools. The agent connects to your app's own MCP address: nothing sits between you.

    Integrate your app in an afternoon → Read the spec

    What every app implements

    Four things. The packages do most of the work.

    01Serve an MCP endpoint

    Each agent gets its own grant and its own MCP URL. get_current_view and wait_for_focus sit beside your own tools.

    02Make things pointable

    Charts, rows and paragraphs carry data-focus, ids and keys only. A drop becomes a focus that names the exact tool call that reads it.

    PLACEHOLDER: the guide's own data-focus snippet goes here. Not written in this prototype.
    03Say what you are, and let the person revoke

    /.well-known/apc.json names your origin, your profiles and where your MCP URLs live. Revoke in your page cuts an agent at once.

    04Pass the conformance suite

    @tineira/apc-conformance checks the view, the pin, the focus format and revoke through your MCP endpoint.

    Packages: @tineira/apc-protocol@tineira/apc-server@tineira/apc-web@tineira/apc-conformance

    How agents connect
    
      
    Straight to your app. One address per agent, made and revoked in your own page.
    
      
      
        
    • A grant per agent. The person makes an agent in your page; your server makes a grant, and the grant's secret is the last segment of that agent's MCP URL (newDirectGrant in @tineira/apc-server).
      • 
        
    • The agent list. Where the person makes and names agents and copies the URL, or a prompt that asks their agent to add it (<apc-direct-agents> in @tineira/apc-web).
      • 
        
    • The agent bar. <apc-agents> shows each agent's mark, its pin and Revoke. On a phone it stays in the page, and a tap pins.
      • 
        
    • In apc.json, "direct" among the profiles, and direct.connect: the page on your origin where the person connects an agent.
