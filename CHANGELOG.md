# Changelog

This is the changelog of the software. The APC standard (the contract an app and an agent share) is versioned apart, with its own [changelog](packages/protocol/CHANGELOG.md). Every PR that changes behavior adds an entry under `## Unreleased`; see `AGENTS.md`.

## Unreleased

Everything that merged after `v0.2.0` (PRs #16 to #109) and has not been tagged yet. APC v2 (the dock, Signals, the extension and the desktop apps) was built in this stretch; v1 at apc.tineira.com keeps working as in 0.2.0.

### Chart readings use only values the page writes (2026-11-09, TIN-765)

- **The browser extension no longer estimates a chart mark's value from where it is drawn: a point or bar reads its value only from its own accessible text or a data label drawn at it, and otherwise says so, as in "SMU · S (no value written on the chart)"; a sales bar on a chart with a second (price) axis no longer reads "≈ 4.12k".** No chart library is known any more (no class name is read): marks, axes, the plot, outline-copied labels and HTML labels over a drawing are told by paint, size, place, `aria-hidden` and roles; a series is named only by its group's `aria-label` or the single title of a one-series chart; a data label is a value only when one mark alone can own it (never in a gutter, on a column behind a point, or as a stack's total); chart labels are read only as the person sees them inside the chart, never from a control; a pie's slice or a map's region with its own `aria-label` is a mark read from that name; a corpus of hand-written Highcharts-, ECharts-, D3- and Recharts-like pages, a canvas chart, named and unnamed pies and maps, a big table and a form is checked in Chrome. (`apps/extension/src/chart.ts`, `apps/extension/src/content.ts`, `apps/extension/test/chart.test.ts`, `scripts/extension-e2e.mjs`, `scripts/extension-e2e-corpus/`, `DESIGN.md`)

### A dock agent's drop that an app will not take always gets an answer (2026-11-09, TIN-756)

- **Dropping a dock agent on a target of an APC app that takes no native drops (Pulse without `?apc=v2`) now shows the extension's "This page did not take your agent here" card, instead of nothing; and when an app answered the drag and then refuses the drop (`canTarget` says no, or it does not know the agent), it tells the person through the app's own `onDockUnsupported` (a note on the page when it has none), with the new `reason: "refused"` on `DockUnsupported` in `@tineira/apc-web`, a value an app that switches on `reason` should handle; Pulse and Signals show it in their own banner without leaving the screen.** The extension's rule for "the app takes this drop" is still the app's own (`appTakes`); it now also looks at whether the app answered the last dragover over a target. The extension's end-to-end run waits for the pin's node again instead of using an id the extension had already replaced (apps/extension/src/content.ts, apps/extension/src/drop.ts, packages/web/src/pinning.ts, scripts/extension-e2e.mjs)

### A block's heading is its own box's title, also when that title is a dropdown (2026-11-09, TIN-766)

- **When the browser extension reads a drop on a row or a table, the heading it gives is now the title of the block's own box, even when the box is titled by a dropdown: a row of a per-retailer table reads "Under the heading: (I) Retailer", not the title of the table next to it.** A box is titled by a heading, then by the control at its top (never on the page as a whole, so a tenant select in a header titles no panel, and after an unnamed icon; a `select`'s chosen option, a `role=combobox` or a listbox button's shown value, never the other options, a typed field, or a control that holds a secret), then by its own `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label` (one rule for both: not a row, cell, list item, `main`, `header`, `footer`, SVG shape or generic role, and only when shown), before the previous heading in the page's order is used (`apps/extension/src/read.ts`, `apps/extension/src/content.ts`, DESIGN.md 13.2).

### Thai, Lao, Khmer and Burmese text cut by a one-line box is cut to what shows, like CJK (2026-11-09, TIN-753)

- **When the browser extension reads a word of Thai, Lao, Khmer or Burmese (up to 32 units, on one line) that the edge of a scroll box cuts, it now gives only the characters that show, instead of the whole word with the part the person cannot see.** These scripts have no spaces between words, so they are treated like Han, Kana and Hangul; a Latin word of the same length still stays whole, and a combining mark is never split from its letter. (`apps/extension/src/read.ts`, `apps/extension/test/read.test.ts`, `DESIGN.md`)

### A run of percentages or amounts is not a card number (2026-11-09, TIN-762)

- **The hub's check for card numbers in visible text no longer takes a run of figures for one: when sensitive numbers are left out (Ask per drop and Always leave out), a whole-chart reading of "98,51% 96,23% 90,42% 87,67% 94,81%" (spaced or not) or "$293.460.353 $332.718.794" goes whole, instead of "98,[sensitive number left out],81%".** A card is now one grammar of how it is written: an unbroken run, digit by digit (single digits with only spaces or dashes between them), or groups of at least 4 digits of which only the last may be shorter, joined as before (a word or a unit such as "USD" or "kg" still joins, as letters do) but never across a percent, per-mille or currency sign, 13 to 19 digits, a card network's start and the Luhn check; what is left out is the union of every span that is a card, so a 19-digit card whose first 16 digits pass Luhn, or a card after a 4-digit figure, no longer keeps its last digits in the text, and every card form the earlier reviews found is still left out. (`apps/dock/src/worker/reading.ts`, `apps/dock/test/reading.test.ts`, `DESIGN.md`)

### A drop on a chart's point or bar points at that mark (2026-11-09, TIN-760)

### The landing's clip plays in Safari and on iOS (2026-11-04, TIN-623)

- **The dock answers byte-range requests for `/media/*` (the hero clip and its poster), so Safari and iOS, which only play a video served in parts, can play it.** Workers static assets answer `Range` with the whole file as `200` and no `Accept-Ranges` (the same locally under `wrangler dev`, so it is the asset layer, not the zone cache); `/media/*` now runs the Worker first, which fetches the file from `ASSETS` and answers one range (`bytes=a-b`, `a-`, `-n`) with `206`, `Content-Range` and `Content-Length`, a range it cannot satisfy with `416` and `Content-Range: bytes */size`, several ranges or none with the whole file and `Accept-Ranges: bytes`, and `HEAD` the same without a body. ### The outline is what goes, and a setting for sensitive numbers (2026-11-04, TIN-619, TIN-617)

- **A page reading is what the outline covered when the agent was let go, including every field the person can see in it and what was typed there.** On a Google Form a drop on the "Email" question's card sent its title but not the typed email, since only the field right under the pointer gave its value. Now the outline drawn while dragging is the scope and the consent: a block of text, a field's card (the largest box around a control that holds no other control: the field with its label, a Google Form's question, found from the control, from a `label`, a `legend` or an element the control names in `aria-labelledby`), or a whole form when the drop lands on its margin; the drop and a moved pin read that same element. Every control in it that the person can see goes, with its label: typed values, the chosen radio, checkboxes, a select's option. The 25-control and fieldset scope rules and "only the field dropped on" are gone; the caps stay (20 fields, 200 characters a value, 1,500 in all, "N more fields not included"), and so do the visibility rules. Passwords and one-time codes (and a card's security code) never go, in any mode: "(left out: password or code)". The extension's own cards no longer count as what a drop lands on. (`apps/extension/src/content.ts`, `apps/extension/src/read.ts`, `apps/dock/src/worker/reading.ts`)
- **Sensitive numbers follow one setting, in the popup and on the welcome page: Ask per drop (the default), Send if it's in the outline, or Always leave out.** Under Ask per drop, card, account and id numbers (the existing filters) are left out and a card after the drop says "Sent to your agent. N sensitive values left out." with **Include them this time**, which sends the reading taken at the drop again with them; the consent card says what will happen to them. The extension marks each value the person chose to send (`sensitive: "included"`, and on the outlined text and the selection), and the hub keeps only marked ones, withholds the rest as before, and never accepts a password or a code even when marked. In visible text (the outlined block, the selection and its block) a sensitive number is now found by validators, not by counting digits: a Luhn-valid 13 to 19 digit card number starting as the card networks' do, its groups joined by separators or a few letters ("4111, 1111, 1111, 1111", "Pay 4111aaaa1111aaaa…"), a checksum-valid IBAN, or a RUN with a valid check character; only that number is replaced by "[sensitive number left out]", so lists of years, dates, scores and phone numbers stay, and a selection keeps the rest of its words (TIN-617). Each drop's reading has an id; the service worker keeps that reading by its id (session storage, memory only) and "Include them this time" sends only it, once that drop's consent was given. - **The card, the welcome page, the dock's Allow and the tool's text say it plainly, and a site set to Always asks once more.** Everything visible in the outline goes, including what was typed; sensitive numbers per the setting; passwords and codes never; it passes through the APC hub (kept 10 minutes at most) and then to the agent's provider. ### Page readings with form state, and drops on pages that fight the drag (2026-11-04, TIN-571, TIN-573)

- **A page reading carries the choices made where the person dropped their agent in a form, so the agent can tell which shipping option is chosen.** On the drop (or a moved pin), and never while the person types, the extension reads the choices of the pointed control's own group (its fieldset or radiogroup; its form or `role=group` only when that holds at most 25 controls): the chosen radio of each group under its legend, every checkbox checked or not, a select's chosen options, each with its accessible label (`aria-labelledby` resolved in shadow roots too). Of typed text it reads only the field dropped on, marked `(pointed at)`; never another field. A control the person cannot see (an effective opacity under 0.1, of no size, off the page, clipped, `aria-hidden`, or a typed field with transparent text) is never read; only a native radio or checkbox counts as seen through its visible label, and a field dropped on that is not seen says "(left out as sensitive)". Hidden inputs, buttons and unseen controls do not count toward the 25.
