# Changelog

This is the changelog of the software. The APC standard (the contract an app and an agent share) is versioned apart, with its own [changelog](packages/protocol/CHANGELOG.md). Every PR that changes behavior adds an entry under `## Unreleased`; see `AGENTS.md`.

## Unreleased

Everything that merged after `v0.2.0` (PRs #16 to #109) and has not been tagged yet. APC v2 (the dock, Signals, the extension and the desktop apps) was built in this stretch; v1 at apc.tineira.com keeps working as in 0.2.0.

### The 0.10 conformance checks close the reviews' gaps (2026-11-13, TIN-894)

- **M2, M3 and M4 hold on `/apc/confirm` too.** The stand-in hub breaks `/apc/confirm` with `confirmMode`; a drop of a grant in handoff against a redirecting, stalling or oversized confirm answer must refuse it within the deadline and call nothing past the hub. (`packages/conformance/src/hub.ts`, `packages/conformance/src/dock-lifecycle.ts`)
- **M3 measures the whole call.** A new stall (`slow_headers_then_stall`: headers at 90% of the deadline, then a body that never ends) and a time limit of the deadline plus under 30% fail an app whose two deadlines add up past it. (`packages/conformance/src/hub.ts`, `packages/conformance/src/dock-lifecycle.ts`)
- **M6 refuses look-alike origins, counts only the app's own answer to the other-host probe, and fails a GET that makes a consent.** A longer host, another port, a trailing dot, a prefix and a cut-short origin; without `app.fetch`, or when a redirect, a 5xx or a network error answers, the other-host probe is a note; Cancel's GET probe is a note when `body()` pairs no grant during the call. (`packages/conformance/src/dock-lifecycle.ts`, `packages/conformance/src/index.ts`)
- **M1 checks the Allow's hub itself, not `allowCarriesHub`.** The suite posts the Allow with a foreign hub to `owner.allow`; it must be refused or ignored. (`packages/conformance/src/dock-lifecycle.ts`)
- **The skip for an app before 0.10 needs a grant at the owner origin, the T_connect and precedence checks use the app's own `connectMs`, long dormancy is checked with `dormancyMs`, and the suite gives its grants back after each check.** Orders declares its 30-day long dormancy. (`packages/conformance/src/dock-lifecycle.ts`, `packages/conformance/src/index.ts`, `apps/dock/src/docs/examples/orders-conformance.ts`, `apps/dock/src/docs/pages/conformance.md`)

### The reference hub and the reference apps meet APC 0.10 (2026-11-13, TIN-892)

- **The dock's hub sends a grant nothing over MCP before the dock owner approved it with the code, and `initialize` before anything else (H1).** Three guarded wrappers are the only callers of the app client; a pairing settled without its introduction is introduced on first use. (`apps/dock/src/worker/hub.ts`)
- **`/apc/confirm` expires overdue requests before it answers, and `/apc/pair` replaces an unapproved pending request of the same agent and app (H2, H3).** An expired request is `not_paired`; the replaced request is outside the cap, its grant is given back after the answer when the URL differs, and the new one's wrong-code count starts at 0. (`apps/dock/src/worker/hub.ts`)
- **The hub knows when an app is asleep, and relays `pairing` and `idle` as tool errors (H4).** `idle: true` per app in `get_current_view`'s `views[]`; when every paired app is asleep `wait_for_focus` answers the idle shape instead of "the person left", and with a mixed fleet it waits as usual and names the apps asleep (`asleep`). (`apps/dock/src/worker/hub.ts`)
- **A grant that ended at the app leaves an "Ended by" line in the dock, and the agent is told `pairing_ended` (H5).** Never `app_unreachable` or `unknown_app`; the line is outside the cap, replaced by the next drop, and removable. The hub's instructions say what `idle`, `pairing` and `pairing_ended` mean (H6). (`apps/dock/src/worker/hub.ts`, `apps/dock/src/web/agent-page.tsx`, `apps/dock/src/web/hub.tsx`)
- **The hub decides a pairing on an `apc.json` at most a minute old, and refuses when the file is out of reach (H7).** Verified apps too: no fallback to the kept copy or to `APP_ORIGINS`. (`apps/dock/src/worker/hub.ts`, `apps/dock/src/worker/trust.ts`)
- **While the dock is connecting to an app, the request says so and takes no code.** A code sent then is answered `approving` and never counted as a wrong code. (`apps/dock/src/web/pairing-request.tsx`, `apps/dock/src/web/request-state.ts`, `apps/dock/src/worker/hub.ts`)
- **Pulse and Signals gate a dock grant by its state.** In flight answers 404, handoff serves only `initialize`, `ping` and `tools/list` and answers `pairing` to a call, and only the hub's committed `initialize` makes it live; a grant ends 30 s after an Allow that heard nothing and 10 minutes after an acknowledgement with no `initialize`. The apps do not sleep (dormancy is a SHOULD). (`apps/demo/src/worker/sandbox.ts`, `apps/signals/src/worker/feed.ts`, `apps/demo/src/worker/dock-app.ts`)
- **Pulse and Signals pair only with allowlisted hubs, checked on the drop and again on the Allow, and send hub calls to the parsed origin (M1, M9).** (`apps/demo/src/worker/sandbox.ts`, `apps/signals/src/worker/feed.ts`)
- **The person's endpoints check `Origin`, `Sec-Fetch-Site` and the JSON media type against a configured `PUBLIC_ORIGIN` (M6).** Every POST under `/api` and the WebSocket handshake; a client with neither header (Pulse for Windows) is taken on its session. (`apps/demo/src/worker/index.ts`, `apps/signals/src/worker/index.ts`, `apps/demo/wrangler.jsonc`, `apps/signals/wrangler.jsonc`, `scripts/mobile-e2e.mjs`)
- **`/apc/revoke` answers the same 200 to anything, and `apc.json` lists `dock` only while pairing is configured (M7, M8).** The give-back ends a grant in flight, in handoff or live by its secret's hash, and leaves a revoked one revoked. (`apps/demo/src/worker/index.ts`, `apps/signals/src/worker/index.ts`)
- **Cancel on the waiting card is its own endpoint, a revoke allowed only before the dock connected; a second drop before then shows the same code.** `POST /api/s/:sid/hub/cancel`, atomic with `initialize`; `not_paired` or a passed expiry starts a fresh consent, `paired` shows "connecting", a newer drop replaces the consent and the stale Allow gets `consent_expired`. Signals now stores the code and its expiry, and Pulse's page no longer receives a dock grant's secret. (`apps/demo/src/worker/*`, `apps/signals/src/worker/*`, `apps/demo/src/web/*`, `apps/signals/src/web/app.tsx`)
- **The docs' Orders example meets APC 0.10 on the Dock profile, with the `@tineira/apc-server` helpers, and sleeps after 30 minutes without the person.** The integration guide and the conformance page show its code. (`apps/dock/src/docs/examples/*`, `apps/dock/src/docs/pages/integrate.md`, `apps/dock/src/docs/pages/conformance.md`)
- **The hub retries an introduction that failed, and the dock says "connecting" until the app answered `initialize`.** Tries at 15 s, 45 s, 2, 4 and 8 minutes after the approval, inside the app's 10 minutes; a pair refused for the cap keeps the "Ended by" or "Revoked by" line; an app's own `attention`, `then` or `apc_link` on a relayed `pairing` or `idle` error is dropped; with some apps asleep and another's page closed, `wait_for_focus` no longer says "do not wait again". (`apps/dock/src/worker/hub.ts`, `apps/dock/src/worker/trust.ts`)
- **Pulse, Signals and Orders read at most 1 MiB of an MCP request (HTTP 413 past it), and agent traffic on a grant in handoff no longer extends the sandbox or feed.** A Pulse dock grant from before 0.10 that still waited for its code is in handoff, not live, and a dock grant whose link was never written is ended, never served as a direct one. Orders' page takes its heartbeat interval from the app's T_idle. (`apps/demo/src/worker/sandbox.ts`, `apps/demo/src/worker/dock-app.ts`, `apps/signals/src/worker/feed.ts`, `apps/dock/src/docs/examples/orders-app.ts`, `apps/dock/src/docs/examples/orders-page.ts`)
- **Pulse, Signals and Orders pass all 31 of the conformance suite's 0.10 Dock checks, with no pending list.** (`apps/demo/test/*`, `apps/signals/test/*`, `apps/dock/test/*`)

### Conformance checks the Dock profile as built, APC 0.10 (2026-11-13, TIN-884)

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-19)

- **A customer pin carries no params; a figure pin only the keys its figure reads.** A pin like `{"kind":"customer","id":"cus_0001","params":{"note":"SECRET-1234.57"}}` was stored, logged and handed to the agent; it is now refused with `invalid_target`, and so is a figure pin with an extra key. (`apps/demo/src/worker/domain.ts`)
- **The activity switch also stops the pulse on an agent's mark**, as it already stopped the flash on what the agent read. Marks and pins stay visible. (`apps/demo/src/web/App.tsx`, `apps/demo/src/web/presence.ts`)
- **With view sharing off, agents no longer learn whether the page is open.** No result carries `then`, and `wait_for_focus` waits its full timeout without reporting `page_open` or ending early when the page closes; it still returns a pin dropped during the wait. Nothing changes with sharing on. (`apps/demo/src/worker/sandbox.ts`, `apps/demo/src/worker/page.ts`, `packages/server/src/tools.ts`, `packages/server/src/prompts.ts`, `skills/pulse-agent/SKILL.md`)
- **Canary check:** `node scripts/canary.mjs <events.jsonl> <mrr-by-customer.csv>` reports whether any of the person's MRR values appear in a sandbox's event log, and exits 1 if one does. (`scripts/canary.mjs`, `scripts/lib/canary.mjs`)
- **DESIGN.md corrections:** the envelope for a pin on a monthly bar is 571 bytes, not 401; what `envelope_bytes` and `then_bytes` measure; the 2 s poll and the 75 s fallback window of `wait_for_focus`; the sharing-off behavior; the log's boundary for the canary; the customer-pin allowlist. (`DESIGN.md`)
